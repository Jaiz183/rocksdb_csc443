## 3. Key Takeaways

1. **Secondary Delete & Total Mixed Throughput**: Metadata-only
   `DB::SecondaryDeleteBelow` (`0.57--0.80 ms` per 1,000-key delete, zero
   filter reconstruction) improves delete throughput over `plain_bloom10`
   (`20.89--26.54 ms` scan + tombstone flush) by **`+2,695.5%` to `+4,164.1%`
   (`28.0x--42.6x`)**, driving **`+325.8%` to `+3,563.4%`** higher total
   mixed-workload throughput on `25-75..100-0`.
2. **Point-Query Scaling with Tile Size `h`**: Because `kiwi_page_bloom10`
   probes up to `h` per-page Bloom filters per tile, XOR filter gains grow with
   `h`. Against `kiwi_page_bloom10`, `kiwi_tile_xor_baseline` (Directory v2)
   improves in-memory QPS by **`+8.6%` / `+17.5%` / `+38.5%`** (latency
   **`-7.9%` / `-14.7%` / `-27.8%`**) and I/O QPS by **`+6.4%` / `+10.0%` /
   `+20.2%`** at `h = 4 / 8 / 16`. `kiwi_sst_xor_baseline` (Directory v3,
   probing one SST-wide XOR filter before `NewIndexIterator()`) improves
   in-memory QPS by **`+43.5%` / `+53.5%` / `+78.1%`** (latency **`-30.3%` /
   `-34.8%` / `-43.8%`**) over `kiwi_page_bloom10`, and also beats
   `plain_bloom10` by **`+25.1%` to `+40.4%`** in-memory QPS (`-20.0%` to
   `-28.8%` latency) across every split and tile size.
3. **Write Amplification vs Space Amplification**: Across delete splits
   (`25-75..100-0`), both XOR configurations write **`0.00 MB`** during the
   workload (`plain_bloom10` writes `26.55--54.98 MB`), cutting total `w_amp`
   by **`-23.9%` to `-46.7%`**. In exchange for zero-rewrite deletes on
   partially expired bottommost SSTs, post-workload `sp_amp` before forced
   compaction rises from `1.26x` (`25-75`) to `4.18x--5.08x` (`100-0`) versus
   `1.18x--2.07x` on `plain_bloom10` (parity with `kiwi_page_bloom10` within
   `-3.8%` to `+4.4%`).
4. **Memory Consumption**: Replacing per-page Bloom filters with bit-packed
   3-way XOR+page filters cuts `flt_bpk` vs `kiwi_page_bloom10` by
   **`-21.1%` / `-15.6%` / `-9.7%`** (`kiwi_tile_xor_baseline`) and
   **`-25.4%` / `-18.2%` / `-11.0%`** (`kiwi_sst_xor_baseline`), cuts `dir_bpk`
   by **`-8.3%` to `-20.3%`**, and cuts pinned `TableReader` RAM (`tr_KB`) by
   **`-45.2%` to `-49.0%`** before workload (`410.5--439.2 KB` vs
   `774.8--806.1 KB`) and **`-35.7%` to `-56.7%`** after deletes. Relative to
   `plain_bloom10` (`~272 KB`), pinned `TableReader` RAM is `+51.7%` to
   `+61.7%` higher due to `ceil(log2(h))` page-index bits and per-page delete
   fences, while pre-workload SST disk size differs by **`<= +1.2%`**.
